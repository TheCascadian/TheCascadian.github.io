About

I’m a game developer and UI/UX designer who spends a lot of time building tools, modding games, and turning small ideas into much larger projects. I keep coming back to simulation, history, procedural systems, and interfaces.

Complex systems should still be readable. Interfaces should feel good to use. Tools should stay out of the way. Games should respect the player’s time. And if something can be moddable, I would rather it be moddable.
