Universal Armor, extended. Build armor from four materials at the **Armory Station**, pick a shape, and preview it in 3D before you craft.

***

## What It Adds

*   **Armory Station.** One labelled slot per part (frame, padding, plating, attachment), a live 3D preview, cost and stat readouts, and an in-game guide.
*   **Three shapes.** Light, standard and heavy each have their own texture layout. Heavy also has its own 3D model with a crest, pauldrons, keel, toe caps and tassets.
*   **Shape trade-offs.** Heavy armor is slower, light armor is faster.
*   **Armory Guide.** A craftable book that explains parts, shapes and costs.

Requires NeoForge 1.21.1. Early development (0.0.1). MIT licensed.

***

## Building

```bash
git clone https://github.com/TheCascadian/Universal-Armor-Extended.git
cd Universal-Armor-Extended
./gradlew build
```

The jar is written to `build/libs/`. Design tooling for the shape textures and the logo is in `design/`.
